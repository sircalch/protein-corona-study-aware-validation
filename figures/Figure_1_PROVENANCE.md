# Figure 1 provenance

Figure 1 is a project-authored conceptual and data-architecture schematic. It depicts only the frozen public summary values: 595 records, 51 Study ID units, eight recorded categorical predictors, four fixed protein targets, and the frozen macro Brier-skill-score values. It is not a molecular simulation, microscopy image, or reconstruction of source data.

The assets supplied here are SVG and PDF vector originals plus 600 dpi PNG and TIFF exports. The underlying frozen values are represented in the repository aggregate outputs and configuration files.
